With the previous stories implemented (except for inverse bosonic fourier transforms), we should have all the machinery we need to perform a self-consistent GF2 calculation.
This is a precursor to a full GW calculation.

We can represent Green's functions right now on a tau grid or on a matsubara grid for both imaginary time and imaginary frequency representations.

The remaining machinery:
Dyson equation solving Σ->G
Self-energy evaluation: G->Σ


## Dyson equation
The Dyson equation is point-local in Matsubara frequency space, but a convolution in tau-space.
It makes much more sense to be in Matsubara frequency space to solve this equation.
